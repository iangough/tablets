Every day

Nothing. The app assumes you take every morning and evening dose, and the stock falls on its own. Open it when you want to see how many days are left.

Colours on the Stock tab: green plenty, amber order soon, red means order now. "Order by" is the last sensible day to ask Patient Access.

Adding and changing tablets

On the Stock tab tap ＋ Add a tablet. Fill in the name, tap the type (tablet, capsule, liquid, boxes), the morning and evening dose, the box size, where it comes from (prescription, bought in the UK, bought abroad) and how many you have now. Tap any tablet, then Edit this tablet, to change it or delete it. You can also import a CSV (see below).

When something goes wrong

Open Log, pick what happened, save. A banner confirms it at the top.

  • Missed a dose: choose the tablet (or All), the date and morning, evening or both. The tablets stay in stock.
  • Recount: if the number you hold differs from the app, type the real number. This is the quickest way to fix any drift.
  • Paused, took extra, moved between places: same screen.
  • Made a mistake? Delete the line in History with the ✕.

Bags from the pharmacy: Bulk receipt

  1. Tap the Receive tab. You see one tablet at a time, starting with anything you ordered.
  2. Tap + 1 box once per box in the bag. For part boxes use the big − and + buttons, which move one tablet at a time (hold to go faster), or + ½ box, or type the exact number.
  3. The card shows ordered, already received and expected. It says "still owed 15" or "order complete".
  4. Use Next and Back, or swipe, to move on. The last page lists everything; tap Save receipt.
Part deliveries: if the pharmacy gives 45 of 60 and owes 15, save as normal. The order stays open as "owed". When the rest arrives, receive it the same way and the order closes. Undo on the last page reverses a receipt.

Ordering

The Orders tab lists what is due. After you order in Patient Access, tap I ordered it. Mark Approved or Rejected when you hear. A rejection is flagged on the Alerts tab.

Trips to 24B

Add your dates in Settings → trips. Before you fly out, Alerts shows what would run low while you are away, so you can order and collect first. Three or four days before you leave, it reminds you to buy anything you can only get abroad.

Importing and exporting a CSV

People, PINs and sharing

  • Each person has their own list, protected by their own PIN (4 digits). Choose it when you start your list, or later in Settings. It backs the list up and lets you open it on another phone, an iPhone or the web.
  • To let someone see your list, give them your PIN. They go to Settings → Open another list and type it. You and your partner can each open the other's list. Nobody else can see anything, and the public web link contains no medical information.
  • A name chip at the top switches between the lists on a phone. The Alerts tab shows alerts from every list on that phone, with the person's name. This can be turned off.
  • Lost a phone? Settings → Devices that can see this list removes it. Then change the PIN.
  • No internet, or the Synology is down? The app keeps working from the last saved data and shows a small note at the top. Changes made meanwhile are merged when it reconnects.
  • On an iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Optional: Settings → On this device can ask for the PIN each time the app opens. A new phone always asks once.

Texts and calendar (Android phone only)

  • Settings → Pharmacy holds the pharmacy name, town, phone and the sender name its texts show (for example Day-Lewis). The tick lets you ignore a pharmacy.
  • Settings → Android phone only → Read "ready to collect" texts: the app watches only texts from those senders that say ready, collect or waiting. It keeps no message text. When one arrives you get a notification, your open orders change to Ready to collect, and a "Collect tablets" entry is placed in your calendar.
  • Put orders and collections in my calendar makes short timed entries with a reminder in the first free weekday slot: ordering (15 minutes), collecting before a trip or when a text arrives (30 minutes), and buying something abroad before you leave. You can invite another person by email. Nothing is sent to anyone else's phone, and entries are only made once.
  • On an iPhone or the web these are greyed out. You can still tap Ready to collect on an order yourself.
  • The phone asks permission for texts, notifications and the calendar the first time you switch each on.

Tablets SOS: the emergency screen

A second icon, Tablets SOS, shows the key medical details of the phone's owner to an ambulance crew or doctor: name, allergies, conditions, warnings (for example stents or blood thinners), emergency contacts you can tap to call, your medicines, and your doctor and pharmacy. It needs no PIN unless you turn that on.

  • Settings → Set up what SOS shows is a short step-by-step guide. Each question says why it matters. Everything is optional and each item has a Show tick. Medicines, allergies, conditions and contacts are on by default. NHS number and home address are off.
  • Blood type, organ donor and advance decisions are optional. Only enter a blood type if you are certain.
  • Preview the SOS screen shows exactly what a stranger would see. Copy text for the lock screen makes text to paste into the phone's own Emergency Information (Android) or Medical ID (iPhone), the only part readable on a locked phone.
  • On Android add the icon from Settings → Add Tablets SOS icon. On an iPhone open the SOS page in Safari and Add to Home Screen.
  • Keep it up to date: the screen shows when it was last changed.

Backup and updates

  • Your data lives only on the phone. Settings → Backup → Show my data copies it as text.
  • The icon is on your home screen. Settings → Add Tablets icon asks again if it goes missing.

What it is

An Android app (Pixel 8 Pro) that tracks tablet stock by routine, not by ticking. It tells you when to order prescribed tablets, when to buy supplements, and what to sort before a trip to 24B. Version 1.2, built 22 November 2026.

Rules it follows

  • You take every morning and evening dose unless you log an exception.
  • Each person's list is separate and opened with that person's PIN. Giving someone your PIN is how you share your list with them.
  • Order when days left ≤ 21 = 5 days delivery + 14 days to the next ordering run + 2 spare. All three are editable.
  • It orders from your real stock, not from Patient Access "next issue" dates, and warns when those dates fall after you would run out.
  • Prescribed tablets come from your pharmacy. Supplements you buy yourself only give a warning, never an order.
  • Warnings come before a trip abroad (14 days ahead, urgent in the last 5), so stock covers the trip.

Not built yet

  • Patient Access ordering script (no public API exists for patients, so it drives the website with your own sign-in; dry-run first).
  • Phone notifications, texts from the pharmacy marking an order ready, and calendar entries for ordering and collecting.
  • Sync between the phone and the laptop, and the laptop version.
  • Bulk-buy price search for supplements (up to 5 boxes).
  • Later release: rotate older tablets, which needs expiry dates.
